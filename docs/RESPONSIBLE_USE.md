# Responsible Use

## Public access is not evidentiary proof

This toolkit helps preserve and organise public research material. It does not authenticate the underlying record for court use.

Researchers should cross-check important facts against the authoritative court record and preserve the procedural posture of the source.

## CAPTCHA

The toolkit does not solve, bypass or outsource CAPTCHA.

Where an official court service requires CAPTCHA, the user performs that interaction manually.

## Rate and access controls

Do not use this project to evade technical controls, overwhelm public infrastructure or mass-download material in a way inconsistent with applicable rules or service constraints.

## Personal information

Court records may contain phone numbers, addresses, account numbers, names and other identifiers.

Do not automatically republish raw documents or unnecessary identifiers.

For public research outputs:

- minimise identifiers
- prefer neutral case IDs
- link to the public source rather than republishing it
- distinguish allegation from finding
- avoid amplifying irrelevant personal information

## Research claims

A source may contain:

- prosecution allegations
- defence submissions
- witness accounts
- police claims
- prima facie judicial observations
- final judicial findings

These are not interchangeable.
